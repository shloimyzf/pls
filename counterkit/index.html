The problem with the notebook

Every beloved counter runs on the same invisible system: memory.

  • Orders arrive by shout, by text, by “my usual, please.”
  • Regulars run house tabs — kept in a notebook, a notes app, or somebody’s head.
  • At month’s end the numbers don’t reconcile. A regular swears they settled. Another forgot entirely. You absorb the difference, because a relationship is worth more than the discrepancy.
  • The platform alternative? A monthly fee, a percentage of every sale, proprietary hardware, and an onboarding call — enterprise machinery pointed at a twelve-seat café.

Your counter doesn’t need enterprise software. It needs discretion, accuracy, and polish: your menu, your queue, and a tab book with a perfect memory.

Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Setup is five quiet steps: create a free Firebase project, paste six configuration values into one file, publish the rules file, create your admin login, and upload the folder to any static host. The README accompanies you click-by-click, in about fifteen minutes. If you can run the demo, you can complete the setup.

What does it cost to run each month?

Nothing, for most counters. CounterKit carries no subscription; hosting is complimentary on Firebase Hosting, Netlify, and their peers, and Firestore’s free Spark plan absorbs a small counter’s volume with room to spare.

How do house tabs work without guest accounts?

By name — the way your counter already works. A tab is keyed to the name a guest orders under; the ledger records every charge and settlement beneath it. Ask regulars to use one consistent spelling.

Can guests pay online?

Not in v1 — by design. The model is the house tab or pay-at-the-counter, which is how counters built on regulars genuinely operate. The code is plain and documented, ready for a developer to extend later.

Can I change the menu, prices, and look?

Menu and prices: yes — live, from the admin page, with nothing to redeploy. The look and wording: yes — one CSS file with the palette at the top, and your business name and tab policy in one configuration file.

Is my data mine?

Entirely. Everything resides in your Firebase project under your Google account. We never see your guests, your orders, or your tabs.

What do I need to run it?

A free Google account (for Firebase) and somewhere to host static files — Firebase Hosting, Netlify, Vercel, and GitHub Pages all offer complimentary tiers.

How do I pay, and when do I get my download?

Payment is by Bitcoin, arranged personally by email (see “How to order” above). Email the tier you want; you’ll receive a reply with your exact BTC amount at the live rate and the payment address. Send exactly that amount — once it confirms on-chain, the download is emailed to you. Because each order is matched by its unique amount, there is no instant checkout; replies are typically sent within a few hours.

What is the refund policy?
